# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Campus Hub is a community project for UTAMU students, staff and contributors.
We are committed to making participation welcoming, respectful and safe for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, ability, experience or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and patient.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Keep feedback focused on ideas and implementation, not individuals.
- Protect private student information and never share credentials.
- Report safety, harassment or privacy concerns promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized conduct,
deliberate exposure of private information, impersonation, spam and malicious
technical activity are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or close
contributions that violate this policy. Reports should include enough context
to investigate and should not publicly expose sensitive information.

## Reporting

For security or privacy issues, follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). For other
conduct concerns, contact the project maintainers privately through the
repository's issue or contact channels rather than posting sensitive details
in a public issue.
